Onions at 5.5% error. Tomatoes at 22% — and the model admits it.

Farmers in India lose money when mandi prices crash after harvest. So I built MandiLens: a crop price forecaster in pure Java. Four models race in a walk-forward backtest — no look-ahead — and the winner forecasts the next 7 days and names the best day to sell. If prices are falling, it tells you to sell today.

Onion and potato forecast well. Tomato doesn't, and the wide confidence band shows it honestly. A forecast that admits what it can't predict is worth more than one that pretends.

The same Java models run in the browser demo — no backend. Try it with your own mandi CSV.
